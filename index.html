    Connect Spotify

    One-time setup, about 3 minutes. Spotify only lets each website log in a few people, so you create your own free "app key". Your music, your account, nobody else's. Spotify Premium required.

    1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your normal Spotify account. Accept the developer terms if asked.
    2. Tap Create app and fill in:
      App nameCarDash
      DescriptionMy car dashboard
      Redirect URI…
      Press Add next to the Redirect URI, tick Web API, accept the terms and Save.
